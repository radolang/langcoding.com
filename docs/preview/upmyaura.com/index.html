The man behind the craft

Meet Danny.
Feel the difference.

Danny is an exceptional, award-winning independent barber based in Calgary, renowned for precision craftsmanship and a passion for delivering the ultimate grooming experience. With over a decade of expertise, Danny doesn’t just cut hair—he transforms it, taking the time to understand each client’s unique style, face shape, and personality. Dedicated to excellence, he uses only the finest premium products—from soothing lavender-infused towels to top-tier shaving tools and high-quality aftershaves—to ensure every service is a luxurious ritual.

At UpMyAura, Danny elevates every appointment into a personal, meticulous experience where quality, comfort, and style reign supreme. His unwavering commitment to detail, cleanliness, and client satisfaction makes him one of Calgary’s most sought-after barbers. When you sit in Danny’s chair, you’re not just getting a haircut—you’re stepping into a world-class grooming journey designed to make you look and feel absolutely incredible.
