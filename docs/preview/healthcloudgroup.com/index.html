Our design principles

Personal information.
Thoughtful foundations.

Health records deserve careful stewardship. These principles guide the platform we intend to build.

Individual control

Consent, sharing preferences, and clear permissions should sit at the centre of the experience.

Security by design

Planned safeguards include encryption in transit and at rest, strong authentication, and role-based access.

Traceable information

Source documents and audit trails should help distinguish original records from extracted data and generated summaries.

Built for continuity

Resilient storage, backups, and recovery are part of the proposed cloud architecture.

These are intended design principles, not claims of an operational or certified service. Security, privacy, and regulatory requirements will need to be assessed and validated before launch.

Have another question?
Can I use the platform right now?

Not yet. Health Cloud Group is at the concept stage. This website explains our vision; it is not a live health-record service. You can register interest or share feedback by email. A launch date has not been announced.

What kinds of health records are envisioned?

The concept includes lab and blood test results, X-rays and other medical imaging, reports and PDFs, heart rate and blood pressure, sleep and nutrition information, and records from dental, vision, hearing, physiotherapy, chiropractic, and specialty providers. Actual support will depend on development and available integrations.

Will AI diagnose me or replace my clinician?

No. We are exploring AI-assisted organisation, summaries, and trend insights to support understanding and conversations. These capabilities would require evaluation before use and would not replace a qualified healthcare professional’s advice or diagnosis.

How much will it cost?

A consumer subscription model is under consideration, but launch pricing and plans have not been set. We’re first focused on validating the needs of individuals and providers.

How can providers, partners, or investors get involved?

We welcome early conversations about clinical workflows, integration, collaboration, and the business concept. Use the enquiry form below to prepare an email, or contact healthcloud@thenightreception.com directly.

Should I send my health records when I get in touch?

No. Please keep enquiries to general questions and feedback. Do not send medical records, test results, or other sensitive health information through this website or the enquiry email.
